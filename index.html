R.A.K.E

drumming to live electronics

R.A.K.E is the solo project of Julien Bloit drumming to live electronics with a minimal second-hand setup. It’s born piece by piece from a daily 20-minute morning ritual where urgency meets trance, repurposing awkward kinds of electrons, raving along kaleidoscopic entropy.

R.A.K.E’s music is rooted in Julien’s practice as a drummer in various garage rock and progressive jazz bands. The result is a raw pulsating sonic matter raging along live-played straight-ahead beats.

  • Genre techno · rock · industrial
  • For fans of Emptyset · Container
